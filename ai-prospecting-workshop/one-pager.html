Your Task

Five Questions · Answer Before Drafting

  1. What happened in the last 12 months? An event with a date, not a description of the company.
  2. What does it operationally force? "...which means their technology function is likely dealing with..."
  3. Hard or routine for this org? Have they integrated at this scale before? What did you check?
  4. What is different about this instance for this company? True of any merging REIT? Then it does not count. No answer? Change angle.
  5. What number or commitment did leadership state publicly that this puts at risk?
