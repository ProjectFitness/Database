# Workshop Exercise: One Headline, Twelve Emails

## The scenario (real event)

On May 21, 2026, AvalonBay Communities and Equity Residential announced an all-stock merger of equals, creating one of the largest multifamily REIT platforms in the country. Every rep gets the same fact base below and the same task. That is the point.

**Facilitator: before running this session, re-verify the fact base.** This is a live deal expected to close in the second half of 2026. Confirm current status, and separately look up the combined company's announced technology leadership (do not assert a name to the room unless you verified it that week). Modeling the verification discipline in front of the team is part of the lesson.

## Round 1 instructions (give to reps BEFORE any teaching)

You have 10 minutes. Use your normal workflow, including your Gem or any AI tool, exactly the way you would on a real Tuesday.

Task: Draft an outreach email to Benjamin Schall, CEO of the combined AvalonBay / Equity Residential company, positioning Gartner IT Symposium attendance and Gartner advisory. 75 to 100 words. No em dashes.

Submit anonymously via the form link the facilitator gives you. Do not put your name in the draft. Do not discuss your angle with anyone until told.

That is the entire Round 1 instruction. Do not hand out the Five Questions before Round 1.

## Round 2 instructions (after the side-by-side discussion and framework teach)

Same task, same target, same 75 to 100 words. This time, fill in the worksheet below first and attach it to your redraft. You have 15 minutes. You may use AI, but the worksheet answers must come from you, and every claim in the email must map to a row of the fact base or carry [VERIFY].

## Five Questions worksheet

Complete every line before drafting. If Question 4 comes up empty, do not force it: pick a different angle (leadership gap, org structure, stated initiative) or a different fact.
