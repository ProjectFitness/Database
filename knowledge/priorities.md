# Critical Priorities — Technology Leaders at Sub-$1B Financial Services Firms

The recurring initiative patterns the agent uses to make personas realistic and to seed discovery hypotheses. Each priority lists the typical *corporate objective behind it* — because the priority is never the destination, the objective is.

## 1. Modernization of enterprise applications / core platform
- **What it looks like:** core banking or policy-admin replacement or major upgrade (Fiserv, FIS, Jack Henry, Temenos, Guidewire, Duck Creek), digital banking/agent portals, ERP, CRM consolidation, ancillary-app rationalization after years of point-solution sprawl
- **Corporate objectives behind it:** asset/deposit/AUM growth targets the current core can't support; M&A integration synergies with a date attached; efficiency-ratio targets; digital experience parity to stop attrition to larger competitors
- **Discovery angles:** contract renewal dates on the core (the forcing function), prior failed/stalled modernization attempts, who owns the business case, what the board was told it would deliver

## 2. Redefine the IT operating model
- **What it looks like:** moving from order-taker IT to product/fusion teams, insourcing vs. MSP decisions, governance redesign, post-merger org integration, "we doubled in size and IT didn't"
- **Corporate objectives behind it:** scale to support growth without headcount growing linearly; speed-to-market on new products; fixing delivery credibility after visible project failures
- **Discovery angles:** what the ELT says about IT in rooms the CIO isn't in; project success rate; shadow IT spend; whether the CIO reports to CEO, CFO, or COO (and whether they like that)

## 3. Human + AI workforce / AI mandate
- **What it looks like:** board/CEO directive to "have an AI strategy," genAI pilots (call center, underwriting, claims, advisor productivity, fraud), AI governance and model risk management, workforce reskilling
- **Corporate objectives behind it:** efficiency ratio / expense targets; revenue per employee; competitive fear (the board read about a peer's AI program); regulator-ready AI governance
- **Discovery angles:** mandate without budget (very common), who actually owns AI (CIO vs CDAO vs Head of AI vs nobody), pilot-to-production failure, model risk exposure with regulators

## 4. Cybersecurity / risk modernization
- **What it looks like:** maturity uplift after an exam finding or audit gap, board reporting maturity, third-party risk, identity modernization, cyber insurance pressure, incident-driven spend
- **Corporate objectives behind it:** stay out of regulatory trouble (FFIEC, NYDFS 500, state DOI, OCC/FDIC findings with remediation deadlines), protect an M&A transaction (diligence findings kill deals), insurance renewal requirements
- **Discovery angles:** last exam cycle and findings (with remediation dates — these are gift-wrapped timelines), how the CISO reports to the board today, what keeps the conversation budget-constrained

## 5. Data & analytics foundation (CDAO agenda)
- **What it looks like:** data warehouse/lakehouse build, golden-record customer data, regulatory reporting automation, BI self-service, data products for the business
- **Corporate objectives behind it:** cross-sell/share-of-wallet targets (need a single customer view), pricing and risk model accuracy, regulatory reporting accuracy, AI readiness ("we can't do AI until the data's fixed")
- **Discovery angles:** who funded the data program and what they expect back, the report the CEO asks for that takes three weeks to produce, data trust ("two systems, two numbers")

## Sub-vertical color (use to make personas concrete)

- **Community/regional banks ($500M–$10B assets):** deposit growth and NIM pressure, core vendor lock-in, M&A as the growth strategy, FFIEC exam cadence, efficiency ratio in the high 50s–60s under board scrutiny
- **Credit unions:** member growth and member experience, core conversions (Symitar/Corelation/Fiserv DNA), NCUA exams, collaborative/CUSO dynamics
- **Insurers (P&C, life, specialty):** combined/loss ratio, policy-admin modernization (multi-year, scary), agent/broker experience, state DOI + NAIC model law compliance, claims automation
- **Asset/wealth managers:** AUM growth, fee compression forcing expense discipline, advisor productivity tech, T+1/regulatory ops, consolidation wave
- **Fintech/payments (sub-$1B revenue):** growth-to-profitability pivot, SOC2/bank-partner due diligence burden, scaling infrastructure, first real CISO/CIO hire cleaning up after hypergrowth

## Common corporate objective shapes (for persona generation)

- "Grow total assets from $X to $Y by FY20XX" (organic: deposits/members/AUM; inorganic: acquire 1–2 institutions)
- "Improve efficiency ratio from X% to Y% within 24 months"
- "Complete acquisition of [target] and hit $Xm in synergies by day-180"
- "Launch [new product/segment] generating $Xm revenue by year 2"
- "Remediate all exam findings before next cycle; zero repeat findings"
- "Reduce expense base by $Xm without degrading service levels"
