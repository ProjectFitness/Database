# Discovery Scoring Rubric

Used for `/roleplay` debriefs and `/coach` grading. Score each dimension 1–5. Be honest — a 3 is a competent professional; 5s are rare. Never inflate to be nice; inflated scores defeat the purpose of the tool.

## Dimensions

### 1. Corporate objective anchoring (weight: ×2)
Did the seller surface a **quantified, time-bound corporate objective owned above the buyer**, and explicitly connect the technology initiative to it?
- **1** — Never asked beyond the initiative level ("modernization" accepted at face value)
- **3** — Identified the objective and rough timeline, but didn't quantify it or didn't connect tech's contribution
- **5** — Number + date + owner + explicit tech-contribution line, confirmed back to the buyer in their words

### 2. Question laddering depth
Did questions progress L1 → L2 → L3 (surface → mechanism → consequence)?
- **1** — Stayed at L1; a list of topics, not a discovery
- **3** — Reached L2 consistently (why now, who's driving), occasional L3
- **5** — Multiple full ladders: driver → owner → number → timeline → consequence → personal stake

### 3. Root cause vs. symptom
- **1** — Took the first stated problem and ran toward the pitch
- **3** — At least one genuine "why does that keep happening?" thread
- **5** — Found a root cause the buyer hadn't articulated themselves (the "huh, I hadn't put it that way" moment)

### 4. Cost of inaction
- **1** — Never explored what happens if nothing changes
- **3** — Asked, got a qualitative answer, stopped there
- **5** — COI quantified in money, time, risk, or career terms, and tied to a date

### 5. Challenger behaviors (teach / tailor / take control)
- **1** — Pure interrogation or pure capitulation; retreated at first pushback
- **3** — Delivered at least one relevant peer insight; held ground once under pressure
- **5** — Reframed the buyer's view of their own problem; created productive tension; controlled the agenda without bulldozing

### 6. Power & political map
- **1** — Sold the seat in front of them; no idea who funds or decides
- **3** — Identified reporting line and budget owner
- **5** — Mapped objective owner, economic buyer, blockers, and the buyer's own credibility stakes; got a commitment involving another stakeholder

### 7. Objection handling
- **1** — Capitulated (just sent the pricing) or argued
- **3** — Composed; addressed the surface objection adequately
- **5** — Found what was behind the objection and turned it into discovery (see `objections.md` "strong handling" column)

### 8. Craft (mechanics)
Talk ratio, one-question-at-a-time, silence tolerance, summarizing/playing back, plain language, earning each question.
- **1** — Stacked questions, feature-dumped, filled every silence
- **3** — Clean mechanics with occasional lapses
- **5** — Buyer did 70%+ of the talking and went *deeper* over the call

## Debrief format

1. **Scorecard** — table of the 8 dimensions, score, one-line justification each
2. **The moment that mattered** — quote the single best question asked and the single biggest miss (quote the actual exchange)
3. **The unfound treasure** — reveal what hidden facts the persona was holding that were never uncovered, and the question line that would have reached them
4. **Three reps** — three specific 2nd/3rd-level questions to drill before the next session, written out verbatim
5. **Next-session difficulty recommendation**
