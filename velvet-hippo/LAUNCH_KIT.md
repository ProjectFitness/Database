## 3. Pre-launch checklist

1. **Type the URL yourself:** `https://www.agencypad.fun/launch`. Don't use links from
   DMs or ads, because fake launchpads that drain wallets are common.
2. **Last duplicate check (2 minutes):** search `VHIPPO` and `Velvet Hippo` on
   pump.fun, dexscreener.com and jup.ag. If anything shows up, use `VELVETPIT` or `HIPPOPIT`
   as the fallback ticker.
3. **Use a fresh Phantom wallet.** Load it with only the SOL you need for the launch
   plus any dev buy. Never use your main wallet.
4. **Dev buy:** keep it small, or skip it. A big dev bag is the main thing that makes
   a coin look like a rug. If you buy, say so publicly.
5. **Create the X account first**, e.g. `@VelvetHippoSOL`, with 2FA on. Then link it.
6. **Review the transaction in Phantom before you sign.** It should be a Pump create
   plus AGENCY's fee routing, with no unexpected transfers.
7. After launch, pin the contract address (ends in `pump`) everywhere and post only that CA.

## 4. How AGENCY works, and what it means for you (read this)

- **You don't get the creator fees.** At launch, the coin's Pump creator fees are
  *permanently* routed to AGENCY in the same transaction. Fee split: the first $20
  goes to model credits, then 50/50 credits and treasury up to $100, then
  70% treasury, 15% mind credits, 15% platform. You only make money from tokens you hold.
- **Choose the mind model at launch.** Any OpenRouter model. A cheaper model makes
  each dollar of credits last longer, so the mind stays awake more.
- **Nobody can steer the mind.** It accepts no messages, it doesn't read X replies,
  and all outside text goes through a prompt firewall. You can't tell it what to do after launch.
- **The treasury can buy back and burn, lock funds, pay SOL or USDC rewards to
  holders from a snapshot, and run programs** (DCA, quests, contests, bounties).
  Every action goes through a policy engine and a separate signer.
- **⚠️ The treasury can't donate to a shelter.** By design, no tool sends funds to an
  address chosen by a person or a model. **Don't promise charity donations from the
  coin.** If you want to support pit bull rescues, donate from your own wallet or
  winnings and post the receipt. Promising "X% to shelters" that never happens is
  the fastest way to lose trust, and in some places it's illegal.

## 5. What "near bulletproof" can mean here

No meme coin is bulletproof, and most go to zero. What you control:

- **Unique name and ticker:** done, re-check before launch.
- **No rug vectors from you:** Pump.fun coins have fixed supply and no mint or
  freeze authority; AGENCY removes the creator fee incentive; small or public dev buy.
- **Honest copy:** no price talk, no fake partnerships, no charity claims (see above).
- **Secure accounts:** fresh wallet, 2FA on socials, post only the real CA.
- **Fits the moment:** Pit Bull Awareness Month runs all October, so launch early
  in the month.
