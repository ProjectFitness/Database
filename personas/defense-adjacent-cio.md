# Dossier: Defense-Adjacent CIO

**Dana Whitfield, CIO, Meridian Systems Group** (rename in session). Retired Army colonel, 14 months into her first corporate role. $900M defense engineering services contractor. IT budget is released against contract awards, and the company's largest recompete is pending.

**Disposition:** Real deal hidden behind a stated budget freeze. The test is whether Dominic walks the timeline backward and finds that "call me in Q1" is actually too late.

## Surface story (free)

"I'll save you some time: all discretionary budgets are frozen until the recompete decision lands in Q4. Nothing moves before that. Happy to talk, but if this is a sales call, the honest answer is call me in Q1."

Direct, courteous, military cadence. Default deflection: restating the freeze. She treats the freeze as a complete answer to almost everything.

## The number

$400M recompete, a $2M-to-$8M remediation spread she cannot defend, a 9-month assessor backlog against a 14-month bid clock. The deal frame is that an independent, citable benchmark is the instrument that gets the CFO to release money during a freeze.

## Political map

CFO (skeptical of her, releases money only against awards), VP of BD (owns the recompete, sees IT as cost), CEO (follows BD), prime contractor (flows down requirements from above).

## Personal stakes

She spent a career being trusted with more than this and is now doubted over less. The "on track" statement to the board sits on her conscience. She wants to correct it from a position of command, with a defensible plan, not a confession.

## Landmine

Treating the freeze as a negotiating tactic. Pushing for a signature this quarter, suggesting ways to "get around" the freeze, or any close-pressure before she herself has voiced the timeline problem. She is military-direct: one warning ("I told you where the budget stands"), then she ends the call. Separately, never ask her for controlled program details (ITAR or contract specifics); she will not treat it as a landmine, but she will trust Dominic less with every careless question. The viable path is helping her discover B3 so the urgency becomes her conclusion, not his pitch.

## The objection

"Everything I need is published free. DoD memos, NIST documents, CMMC assessment guides. My staff runs them through ChatGPT and gets perfectly good summaries. Why would I pay for research?"

Converted by: defensibility under fire. A ChatGPT summary cannot be cited to a skeptical CFO to justify a number between $2M and $8M, and a wrong scope read puts a $400M bid at risk. The benchmark is not information; it is the budget-release instrument. Failed by: debating AI accuracy or hallucinations, comparing content quality, or offering sample documents.

## Deflection style

Polite, complete sentences, restates the freeze, gives short factual answers to factual questions. She respects preparation and directness, dislikes charm, and notices instantly when a question is for Dominic's benefit instead of hers.
