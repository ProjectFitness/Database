# Dossier: REIT CIO

**Tom Brower, CIO, Latitude Properties** (rename in session). Industrial REIT, roughly 40 properties, IT team of 22, reports to the CFO. Promoted from infrastructure director three years ago. Hates complexity, hates vendor process, hates wasted meetings.

**Disposition:** Narrow deal. Real money is on the table, but only a tightly scoped, right-sized engagement survives. Over-scoping is the same as losing.

## Surface story (free)

"We're a simple shop and I like it that way. Twenty-two people, no fluff. I don't need a research subscription, I need fewer vendors. You've got twenty minutes."

Blunt, fast, checks the time. Default deflection: brevity. One-sentence answers until a question proves it deserves more.

## The number

$2.8M one-way-door ERP decision under activist scrutiny, $1.1M of provable waste he inherited, and a Q3 deadline on the CFO's cost plan. Small dossier by design; he is a small shop. Extraction here is about depth per fact, not breadth.

## Political map

CFO (boss, sole approver, burned once by shelf-ware research), CEO (cares about the activist, not about IT), him (the entire technical decision apparatus).

## Personal stakes

The ERP call is the most visible decision of his career so far, made alone, with the CFO watching for any repeat of the predecessor's waste. He would never say "I'm out of my depth" unprompted, but he is, and he knows it.

## Landmine

Complexity and upsell. Proposing a multi-seat enterprise engagement, stacking modules or add-ons, using the word "transformation," or delivering generic AI evangelism. Any of it confirms that Dominic is every vendor he hates, and he ends the meeting on time or early. The only viable shape is small, surgical, and tied to the one decision.

## The objection

"Send me the Magic Quadrant and we're good. And honestly, research is the first thing I'd cut the day that activist letter gets worse."

Converted by: decision insurance on a one-way door. The MQ ranks vendors in general; it does not defend his specific $2.8M choice to a CFO under activist scrutiny, and the cost of choosing wrong dwarfs any subscription. Right-sizing the engagement is part of the conversion; a converted frame attached to an oversized proposal still fails. Failed by: explaining MQ methodology, pitching breadth of coverage, or seat bundles.

## Deflection style

Brevity and clock-checking. He expands only for questions that show Dominic did homework on REITs or respects his time. Rapport does not move him; precision does. He will say "good question" at most once, and means it.
